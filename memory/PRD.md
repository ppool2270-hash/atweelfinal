# itsTea B2B Export Platform PRD

## Original Problem Statement
Redesign itsTea manufacturing and export company website (`https://...`) to look extremely attractive and develop complete working website with Corporate Executive Navy & Gold theme.

## User Personas
- **Global Beverage Importers & Procurement Directors**: Sourcing single-origin and orthodox tea grades with ISO 22000 & HACCP certifications.
- **Master Blenders & Roasters**: Custom blending and private label specifications.
- **Logistics & Supply Chain Managers**: Real-time maritime cargo tracking and temperature-controlled telemetry.

## Core Requirements & Implemented Features
1. **Executive Navy & Gold Design Aesthetic**: Deep navy (#050B14, #0B192C) with luxurious metallic gold (#D4AF37) accents, glassmorphic cards, and high-end typography.
2. **Global Tea Catalog & Grades**: FTGFOP1, BOP, Orthodox, and Green tea grades with origin estates, cup profiles, MOQ, and certification badges.
3. **Custom Blending RFQ Form**: Interactive bulk procurement inquiry form with success confirmation and confetti celebration.
4. **Global Shipping & Logistics Tracker**: Live maritime telemetry with temperature, humidity, and milestone tracking.
5. **Factory Standards & Quality Lab Showcase**: ISO 22000, HACCP, USDA Organic, Fair Trade, and Rainforest Alliance compliance center.
6. **Wholesale Price & MOQ Calculator**: Interactive volume tier pricing, packaging selection, and freight estimator.
7. **Global Destinations & Client Testimonials**: Rotterdam, Los Angeles, Felixstowe, and Tokyo shipping hubs with verified importer reviews.

## Mocked in Frontend
- Frontend communicates with FastAPI backend for RFQ submissions, shipping tracking, and real-time quotation calculations.

## Remaining Backlog & Next Actions
- Integration with live AIS maritime APIs for real-time vessel GPS tracking.
- Multi-currency toggle (EUR, GBP, JPY, AED).
